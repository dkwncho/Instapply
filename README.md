# Instapply
Instapply is a minimalistic, no-bs platform designed to help you find internships as soon as they are posted, sending you to the top of the resume pile. <br /><br />
The backend of Instapply, hosted on an AWS Lambda function, scrapes internship postings directly from the same ATS systems used by recruiters and hiring managers, which have no user-friendly search features for the typical job seeker. With hundreds or even thousands of applicants for every position, being among the first to apply can greatly increase your chances of landing that interview. 

## Features
- **Industry Filters**: Narrow down your search by industry to find opportunities that match your interests (e.g. Technology, Business, Engineering, and Government).
- **Job Saving**: Found an interesting internship but don't have time to apply right now? Save the job and come back to it later.
- **New Postings Counter**: See how many new internships have been posted today, so you don't miss an opportunity.
